- **Hosted DB Inspected (Read-Only):** `duwwlcpdidrxkixvflag` (OpenAPI schema & RPC inventory preflight verified)
- **Confirmed Policy Decisions:**
  - **Services (8A):** Off-platform campus agreement (`requested → accepted → in_progress → delivered → completed` / `declined` / `cancelled` / `expired`); individual request `mode` is strictly `remote` or `in_person` and `preferred_date` is typed `timestamptz`; excluded from paid sales & commission; eligible for **Verified Service** reviews upon completion.
  - **Free Items (8B):** Reuses hosted `public.free_notices` (`active`/`closed`/`hidden`, `already_placed = true`, 1–5 `image_paths`, `spot_id`, `condition`, `expires_at`), `public.donation_spots` (`Blok Alpha`, `Blok Beta`), and `messages.free_notice_id`, adding private `public.free_notice_drafts` (persistent incomplete draft recovery) and `public.free_notice_claims` (atomic quantity reservation, 48h reservation expiry, exact-once release, completed-only impact). Active Students and Staff may claim; Students-only publish via `publish_free_notice_atomic`.
  - **Charts (8E & 9A):** Zero-dependency responsive SVG/CSS charts in Buy-U Campus Pop palette (`#4E2A84`, `#008285`, `#FAF9FC`).
